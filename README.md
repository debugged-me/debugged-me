Software developer at **Softtech Solution and Services Co.** in Mati City, Philippines. I design, build, and maintain production systems for schools and government offices — student records, learning management, attendance and HR, payroll, procurement, and assessment — primarily in **PHP (CodeIgniter / Laravel) and MySQL**.

I also build the **Flutter mobile apps** that sit on top of those systems, a Node.js assessment platform, and native macOS utilities in **Swift** — including **MeowBar**, a menu-bar usage monitor, and **PaperTrail**, a receipt scanning app.
